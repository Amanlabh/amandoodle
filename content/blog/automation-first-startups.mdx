# Automation First Playbook

Early-stage teams move faster when repetitive work is automated.

## What to automate first

- lead intake and routing
- onboarding emails
- release checklists

## Practical stack

Use Next.js + lightweight scripts + hosted workflows.

Keep your process visible and measurable from day one.
